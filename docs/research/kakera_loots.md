# Kakeraloots

## Scope

This document distinguishes two kinds of information:

- **Universal reference knowledge:** every reward that a Kakeraloot can produce, including rewards not yet present on a particular account.
- **Account state:** the specific levels and rewards observed in a copied `$lk` message.

MOA must not infer that an unobserved reward is unavailable. Conversely, it must not treat a universal reward as already owned by an account.

## Directly observed Mudae command evidence

The copied `$infokl` response for Lake Arrowhead 2025 states that Kakeraloots can provide additional rolls, Kakera, disablelist capacity, protected wishes, Mudapins, wishlist slots, improved `$rt`, permanent rolls, Star Branches, and `$bku` bonuses.

The copied `$lk` response exposes account values for stacked rolls, disable limits, Wishprotect, Mudapins, `$rt`, permanent rolls, Star Branches, Quantity, Quality, usage count, and balance.

## Modeling boundary

Quantity and Quality are modeled as account state. The complete reward catalog is modeled as immutable reference data. Exact reward weights, Kakera payout ranges, and therefore Kakeraloot expected value are deliberately absent until independently verified from reliable Mudae data.
